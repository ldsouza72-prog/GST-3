Mission & Solution

Promoting a Circular Economy in Canada.

The Problem

Growing volumes of single-use plastic waste and unsustainable disposal into landfills. Currently, it is estimated that less than 11% of plastic is recycled in Canada. High costs associated with collecting and repurposing disincentivize sustainable operations.

The Solution

We provide a circular economy solution of converting waste into chemicals using solar power. Be part of a sustainable solution to the growing pollution concern and valorize renewable energy and waste polymers for a thriving economy.

"Find an alternative sink for waste plastics using renewable energy to overall reduce the costs associated with recycling."

Our Technology

Innovative Plastic Pyrolysis

Transforming post-consumer and off-spec polymer products into value-added chemicals via an energy-efficient, solar-driven process.

Solar Photovoltaics

Energy harvesting through solar PV arrays minimizes grid reliance. Valorizing solar energy drives the endothermic pyrolysis reactions sustainably and cost-effectively.

Catalytic Pyrolysis Plant

Features a rotary kiln and advanced distillation sections. We adapt game-changing, inexpensive catalysts for highly selective product yields and increased efficiency.

Value-Added Outputs

Input: Polystyrene.
Outputs: Styrene, Benzene, Toluene, and Carbon Black ready for industrial reuse.

Key Differentiators

Renewable Energy Based: Highly energy efficient due to integrated Solar Photovoltaic power.
Advanced Catalytic Method: Delivers a narrow product range with competitive economics and drastic GHG reduction compared to known processes.
